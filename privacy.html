Privacy policy

ghealth personal is a private tool used by a single person to access their own Google Health data.

What data is accessed

With read-only permission, the tool reads the owner's own health and fitness data (such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, activity and exercise) from the Google Health API.

How it is used and stored

The data is saved only to the owner's own computer and the owner's own Google Drive, for personal analysis. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Other users

The tool has no users other than its owner and does not collect data from anyone else.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and exported files can be deleted from Google Drive.

Contact

Questions: the owner's support email as listed on the app's consent screen.
